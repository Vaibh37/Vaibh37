## About Me

I'm **Vaibhav**.

**Rust and C++ are where I sharpen the blade** — systems thinking, performance, ownership, problem solving, and understanding what the machine is actually doing.

When an idea needs to leave the terminal and become something people can use, I reach for **MERN** and ship it.

I learn what I use, build with it, break it, understand it, and come back stronger.
